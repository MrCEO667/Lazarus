07 · CUSTOMS & COMPLIANCE

We own the customs complexity. You don't.

EAEU at a glance

Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan are full EAEU members — once cargo clears either, it moves freely within the union. Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan and Tajikistan are non-members; each requires its own clearance. This map alone saves you a week of clarifying calls.
